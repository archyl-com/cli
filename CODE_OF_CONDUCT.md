# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the archyl CLI to be a project where anyone can participate — reporting a bug, asking a question,
disagreeing with a design decision — without being made to feel unwelcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith and knows things you do not.
- Critique code and decisions, not people. "This query is N+1" is useful; "this is amateur" is not.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A "no" on scope is not a judgement of your work.
- When you disagree, say why, and say what you would do instead.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in the open or in private.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, or level of experience.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate derailing.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **conduct@archyl.com**. Reports go to the maintainers and stay confidential. If
your report concerns a maintainer, say so and it will be handled by the others.

We will acknowledge within 3 business days, look into it, and tell you what we decided.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity: a private word, a public correction, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a
permanent one. We will explain the reasoning to the person concerned.
